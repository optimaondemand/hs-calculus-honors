The Derivative: Definition and Meaning
How do we capture the exact rate of change at a single instant?
📖 WHAT THIS MODULE IS ABOUT
Every idea in Module 1 through Module 3 was building toward one instrument: the derivative. Over these two weeks you will turn a shrinking secant slope into an instantaneous rate of change, learn the formal definition of the derivative at a point, promote that definition into a function that reports the slope everywhere at once, and confront the places where a derivative can fail to exist. This is the last module of Quarter 1 and the doorway into everything that follows.
📅 YOUR LESSONS
WEEK 7
Lesson 4.01 · Average vs Instantaneous Rate of Change
Shrink a secant slope toward the tangent slope.
Lesson 4.02 · The Derivative at a Point
Define \(f'(a)\) as a limit and find the tangent line.
WEEK 8
Lesson 4.03 · The Derivative as a Function
Compute \(f'(x)\) from the definition and read its notation.
Lesson 4.04 · Differentiability and Continuity
Find where a derivative fails to exist, and why continuity alone is not enough.

💡 HOW TO SUCCEED THIS MODULE
Write out every difference quotient by hand before you simplify it. Building the derivative from its definition is slow the first several times and that slowness is the point: it is what makes the shortcut rules trustworthy next quarter. Start the intro survey first and the outro survey last, and govern your own pace across the two weeks rather than rushing to the Module Challenge.

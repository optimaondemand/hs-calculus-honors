Derivative Rules I
Now that you can build a derivative from its definition, what rules let you skip straight to the answer, and why can you trust them?
📖 WHAT THIS MODULE IS ABOUT
Module 4 asked you to build every derivative by hand, straight from the limit definition, so that you would trust what a derivative actually is before you ever shortcut it. This module cashes that trust in. Over the next two weeks you will prove the Constant, Sum, Difference, Power, Product, and Quotient Rules, watch the Power Rule extend honestly from whole numbers to negative and fractional exponents, and build real fluency differentiating polynomial, rational, and radical functions in a single line of arithmetic. The module closes by keeping a promise this course made back in Module 2: proving L'Hôpital's Rule and finally resolving the limit Lesson 2.04 could not. Quarter 2 opens the same way Quarter 1 closed, with proof first and speed only afterward.
📅 YOUR LESSONS
WEEK 9
Lesson 5.01 · The Constant, Sum, and Power Rules
Prove the first three derivative rules from the limit definition and differentiate any polynomial.
Lesson 5.02 · The Product and Quotient Rules
Prove why a product's derivative is not the product of derivatives, and what it actually is.
WEEK 10
Lesson 5.03 · Extending the Power Rule: Rational Functions and Radicals
Extend the Power Rule to negative and fractional exponents, and differentiate any algebraic function.
Lesson 5.04 · L'Hôpital's Rule: Closing the Loop
Prove L'Hôpital's Rule from the derivative and finish the limit Lesson 2.04 left open.

💡 HOW TO SUCCEED THIS MODULE
Work through every proof with your own pencil before you use the rule it proves; a rule you have re-derived once is a rule you actually own, not one you are borrowing. When Lesson 5.03 tells you plainly that one part of the Power Rule is verified rather than fully proved, resist the urge to smooth that over. Naming the exact edge of what you have proved, honestly and out loud, is itself a mathematical skill, and it is the same skill L'Hôpital's Rule asks of you in Lesson 5.04 when you check a form before you ever apply the rule to it.

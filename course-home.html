Welcome to Calculus
The mathematics of change and of the infinitely small.
📖 WHAT THIS COURSE IS
For two thousand years, geometry could measure straight lines and static shapes but stumbled on the moving and the curved. Calculus is the tool humanity finally built to reason about change itself: the exact speed of a falling body at a single instant, the slope of a curve at one point, the area under a curve that no simple formula describes. This year you will learn its two great instruments, the derivative and the integral, and see how a single idea, the limit, makes both possible. The work is demanding and it is worth it. You are joining a conversation that runs from Archimedes through Newton and Leibniz to the mathematics that runs the modern world.
📚 HOW THE COURSE WORKS
▶  The year has four quarters, each with four modules that run two weeks apiece.
▶  Each week has two lessons to work through, one problem set you complete by hand and upload, and one short auto-graded quiz.
▶  Each module includes a discussion, one Module Challenge for your fullest work, and one VR activity.
▶  Start each module with the intro survey and close it with the outro survey. They help your teacher see where you are.
💡 HOW TO SUCCEED
Calculus rewards steady, careful work far more than last-minute effort. Keep a neat notebook, work every practice problem before checking it, and write your solutions vertically with your reasoning shown. When you get stuck, note exactly where, and bring that question to office hours. A little each day beats a great deal all at once.
⚠️ WORTH KNOWING · TOOLS AND YOUR OWN REASONING
Graphing tools and computer algebra systems can compute a limit or a derivative in an instant, and you are welcome to check your work with them. They cannot do the part that matters most in this course: understanding why a method works and choosing the right one. That judgment is what you are building, and it cannot be outsourced. Submit your own reasoning, always.

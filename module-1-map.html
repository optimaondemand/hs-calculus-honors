The Idea of a Limit
How can we speak precisely about what a function approaches, even where it is never defined?
📖 WHAT THIS MODULE IS ABOUT
Every idea in calculus grows from one root: the limit. Over these two weeks you will learn to read what a function approaches from a table and from a graph, evaluate limits with a handful of reliable laws, resolve the puzzling form 0/0, and decide when a two-sided limit exists. Keep your eye on one distinction the whole time: what a function approaches near a point is a separate question from what it equals at the point.
📅 YOUR LESSONS
WEEK 1
Lesson 1.01 · What a Limit Is
Estimate limits from tables and graphs.
Lesson 1.02 · The Limit Laws
Evaluate limits by direct substitution and the laws.
WEEK 2
Lesson 1.03 · When Substitution Fails: the 0/0 Form
Factor and rationalize to resolve 0/0 limits.
Lesson 1.04 · One-Sided Limits
Approach from each side and decide when the limit exists.

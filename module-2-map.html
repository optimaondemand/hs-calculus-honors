Limits Without Bound & Special Limits
What happens to a function at the edges of its domain and where it grows without bound?
📖 WHAT THIS MODULE IS ABOUT
Module 1 asked what a function approaches near a single point. This module pushes the question to the edges: what does a function approach as x runs out toward infinity, and what happens when a function itself grows without bound near a point? You will read end behavior from a rational function's degree, classify infinite limits and vertical asymptotes, trap a stubborn limit between two friendlier ones with the Squeeze Theorem, and give a name to the 0/0 and infinity/infinity forms that keep appearing. None of these ideas resolve quickly. They reward patience with the unbounded and the undecided.

💡 HOW TO SUCCEED THIS MODULE
Infinity is a direction to reason about, not a number to compute with carelessly, and an indeterminate form is a question, not a dead end. Work each lesson's practice in your notebook before moving on, and when a limit resists your first idea, try the next technique rather than guessing. Start the intro survey first and the outro survey last. If a problem stumps you, write down exactly where you got stuck and bring that to office hours.
